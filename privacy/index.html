Privacy & accessibility.

Privacy

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or browser fingerprinting. Its code does not set cookies or store data in your browser. There are no forms, chat services, or third-party embeds. Fonts, images, and scripts are hosted with the site.

The homelab explorer runs in your browser and uses screenshots when 3D is unavailable. It does not collect hardware identifiers or send rendering results to a server. Model downloads link to GitHub or the designer’s original listing.

Animations run on your device. Their timers reset on reload and are never sent to a server.

Cloudflare hosts this site and processes request information, including IP addresses, to deliver its pages. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

If you email me, I receive your address and message so I can reply.

External links lead to websites with their own privacy practices. This site connects to them only when you follow a link.

Accessibility

Use the keyboard to navigate, skip to the main content, switch the Work and Education tabs, and enlarge the portrait. Press Escape to close tooltips or the enlarged photo.

The background animation plays once per page load. Decorative ducks appear after five minutes of viewing and scrolling. With reduced motion enabled on your device, the background animation is skipped, ducks stay still, and the 3D rack does not rotate automatically. Text, project links, and the rack screenshot remain available without JavaScript.

For privacy questions or accessibility issues, email samuelshmidman@gmail.com. Please include the page and what you were trying to do.
