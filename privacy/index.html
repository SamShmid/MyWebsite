Privacy & accessibility.

This page describes privacy and accessibility features of my portfolio.

Privacy

This portfolio’s own code does not set cookies or save preferences or identifiers in your browser. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or device fingerprinting. It has no contact form, chat service, or embedded third-party content. Fonts, images, scripts, and icons are served with the site.

The decorative animations run on your device. Their timing stays in memory, resets when you reload, and is not sent anywhere.

When served through Cloudflare, this site uses Cloudflare to deliver its pages and files. Cloudflare processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide its services. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. Local previews are served from my computer and record basic request logs.

If you email me, I receive your email address and the information you choose to include so I can reply.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and individual projects take you to separate websites with their own privacy practices. No connection to those sites is made by this portfolio until you follow a link.

Accessibility

You can use the keyboard to navigate, skip to the main content, switch the Work and Education tabs, and enlarge the portrait. Press Escape to dismiss a tooltip or reduce the photo.

Use Pause motion at the bottom of the Home or Projects page to pause its automatic animations. The site also follows your device’s reduced-motion preference. The portfolio content remains available with JavaScript turned off.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have a privacy question, email samuelshmidman@gmail.com. For an accessibility issue, please include the page and what you were trying to do.
